Frequently Asked Questions

What is Muslim Space?

Muslim Space is an all-in-one lifestyle app designed to help Muslims stay consistent and connected in their daily worship. The app provides accurate prayer times, a detailed iman tracker, daily thikr, duas, and practical tools to support your spiritual journey.

Is the app free?

Yes, Muslim Space is completely free to download and use. All core features including prayer times, Iman tracking, daily verses, and wellness tools are available at no cost.

How accurate are the prayer times?

Prayer times are calculated using your precise GPS location to ensure accuracy for your specific area. The app uses standard Islamic calculation methods and automatically adjusts for your timezone and daylight saving time.

What is the Iman Tracker?

The Iman Tracker monitors your spiritual activities across three key areas: ʿIbādah (worship), ʿIlm (knowledge), and Amanah (trustworthiness). It provides insights into your spiritual growth and helps you set and achieve goals.

Does the app work offline?

Many features work offline, including saved prayer times, downloaded content, and your personal tracking data. However, some features like location-based prayer times and downloading new content require an internet connection.

How do I get prayer notifications?

After granting notification permissions, the app will automatically send you reminders for each prayer time. You can customize notification settings in the app's settings menu.

Is my data secure?

Yes, we take your privacy seriously. Your personal data is encrypted and stored securely. We never sell your information. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.
